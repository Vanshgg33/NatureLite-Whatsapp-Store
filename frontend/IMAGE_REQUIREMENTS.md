## Photography Guidelines

### Lighting
- Natural daylight or soft studio lighting
- Avoid harsh shadows
- Warm tones (golden hour style) work best with brand colors

### Background
- For PNG images: Pure white background (will be removed)
- For JPG images: Natural textures (wood, marble, linen, jute)

### Props (Optional)
- Raw ingredients (mustard seeds, sesame seeds, groundnuts, coconuts)
- Traditional utensils (brass bowls, wooden spoons)
- Natural elements (leaves, burlap fabric)
- Avoid modern/plastic items

### Color Palette to Match
- Cream: #f5f0e8
- Golden/Mustard: #d4a574, #DAA520
- Brown: #3d2e1f, #8b7355
- Off-white: #e8dfd3

---

## Image Optimization

Before uploading, optimize images for web:

1. **Compress JPGs** - Use TinyPNG or Squoosh to reduce file size
2. **Target file sizes:**
   - PNG files: Under 500KB each
   - JPG files: Under 300KB each
3. **Use correct dimensions** - Don't upload larger than needed
